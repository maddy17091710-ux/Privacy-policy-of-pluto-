Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

Welcome to PLUTO. Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information PLUTO may collect, how it is used, how it is stored, and how you can request its deletion.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, PLUTO may process the following information:

  • Discord user IDs and server IDs.
  • Channel IDs and role IDs.
  • Server configuration, including prefixes, welcome channels, goodbye channels and log settings.
  • Moderation records, including warnings and related moderation information.
  • Security and audit log records.
  • Premium subscription status, activation dates and related server information.
  • Information provided when using commands or contacting our support team.

2. How We Use Information

We use information only as needed to:

  • Provide and operate PLUTO's features.
  • Save server configurations.
  • Provide moderation and security services.
  • Maintain warning and audit records.
  • Manage Premium subscriptions.
  • Provide technical assistance and support.
  • Protect PLUTO against misuse and abuse.

3. Data Storage

PLUTO uses database storage to maintain necessary server settings, moderation records, Premium information and other operational data.

Bot data may be stored on our hosting provider's infrastructure. Discord also processes information under its own Privacy Policy.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell Discord user or server data. We do not share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Information may be processed by service providers such as Discord and our bot hosting provider when necessary to operate PLUTO, or disclosed when required by applicable law.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as it is needed for PLUTO's operation, security, moderation and Premium services, subject to applicable laws.

When information is no longer necessary, it should be deleted or appropriately disposed of.

6. Data Deletion Requests

Users and server owners may request deletion of eligible information associated with their account or server.

To request assistance with data deletion, contact the PLUTO management team through our official support server.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access, misuse and disclosure. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Third-Party Services

PLUTO operates through Discord and uses external hosting infrastructure. These services have their own privacy policies and terms.

Discord Privacy Policy: Read Discord Privacy Policy

9. Children's Privacy

PLUTO is intended to be used in accordance with Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable laws. We do not knowingly seek to collect information from individuals who are below the applicable minimum age.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. The latest version will be published on this page.

11. Contact Us

For privacy questions, data-related requests or assistance, contact our official support team.
